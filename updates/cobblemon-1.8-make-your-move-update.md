# ✨ Cobblemon 1.8 Make Your Move Update

## Make your next adventure count

Cobblemon 1.8 brings new partners to discover and fresh goals for explorers and team builders. Here are the player-facing highlights.

## 🌿 More reasons to explore

**49 habitats, 10 new ruins, and the Magma Shipwreck Cove** expand the world. Habitats blend into their surroundings and provide homes for particular Pokémon; some encounters change from day to day.

Keep habitats intact: their disguised Habitat Blocks control local spawning, and destroying those blocks stops their Pokémon from appearing. Mark interesting places and return later to see what is around.

## 🪽 New riding partners

Crobat, Skarmory, Milotic, Archeops, Goodra, Hisuian Goodra, Drampa, Duraludon, Archaludon, and Pidgeot gain riding support.

## 🛠️ Smaller changes worth knowing

Pokémon now vary naturally in size. The **Move Dex** helps you plan movesets. Porygon gains a Data Monitor acquisition method, hover mounts lose their mining-speed penalty, and Create gains additional Cobblemon processing recipes.

Many models and animations are refreshed. **Advancements reset except starter selection** as part of the update’s cleanup.

_This covers the base Cobblemon update; Hikari Haven’s enabled content, world generation, and settings determine what you encounter here._

## ⚔️ Two big changes for your team

{% column %}
### 👑 Meet Alpha Pokémon

Watch for oversized Pokémon with glowing red eyes. These rare wild bosses scale above your team’s strongest partner, gain battle boosts, and carry TM moves.

Naturally appearing Alphas lead herds of earlier-stage Pokémon. Prepare before approaching: they can defend themselves outside battle, too.

Defeating them can reward training candies, Type Gems, and berries. Finding one is a chance to test your team—or catch a striking new companion.
{% endcolumn %}

{% column %}
### 💿 Build your moveset

TMs teach compatible Pokémon new moves. Unlock recipes by obtaining a Pokémon that knows the move or interacting with a Data Monitor holding its TM.

At a **TM Machine**, choose a recipe and supply a Blank TM, the matching Type Gem, and any extra ingredients shown.

TMs are normally consumed when used. Check compatibility first; an incompatible Pokémon will not consume the disc. You can also discover TMs while exploring ruins.
