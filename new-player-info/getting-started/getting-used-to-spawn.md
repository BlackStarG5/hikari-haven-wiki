# Getting used to Spawn

### Welcome to the neighborhood

Your adventure begins in a hand-built town full of little details to discover. Take your time wandering the streets, get familiar with your surroundings, and enjoy a first look at life in Hikari Haven.

### Explore the town center

The mall sits at the heart of Spawn and makes a handy landmark while you learn your way around. Start there, then branch out along the surrounding streets. You do not need to rush straight into the wilderness—there is plenty of atmosphere to take in before heading out.

### A town with life around every corner

Look out for Pokémon roaming naturally as you explore, and other players hanging out and having fun. Say hello, bring a friend along for a stroll, or ask the community for a little guidance. Spawn is a place to meet fellow trainers as well as the starting point for your own adventure.

### Before you head out

* **Read the Server Rules.** Get familiar with the expectations for claims, battles, trading, and shared spaces.
* **Respect the town.** Leave builds and decorations as you found them, keep paths clear, and ask before using anything that belongs to another player.
* **Explore at your own pace.** Get your bearings, meet a few neighbors, and decide what you want your first adventure to be.
