# ⌨️ Basic Commands

### Everyday essentials

Type commands into Minecraft chat. Replace anything in angle brackets with your own value, without typing the brackets.

* `/spawn` — Return to Spawn.
* `/sethome` — Set a home at your current location.
* `/home` — Teleport to your saved home.
* `/tpa <player>` — Ask to teleport to another player. They must accept your request.

### Travel between worlds

* `/server blackcity` — Travel to Black City. Legacy-player whitelist access is required.
* `/server whiteforest` — Travel to White Forest.
* `/server thewild` — Travel to The Wild.
* `/end` — Visit the End.
* `/nether` — Visit the Nether.

**Remember:** Items and Pokémon carry over between realms, but money does not.

### Other useful commands to try

These are common server commands; availability, exact syntax, cooldowns, and limits may vary on Hikari Haven. Check in-game command suggestions or ask staff if one is unavailable.

* `/tpaccept` — Accept a pending teleport request.
* `/tpdeny` — Decline a pending teleport request.
* `/tpahere <player>` — Ask another player to teleport to you.
* `/sethome <name>` and `/home <name>` — Save and visit named homes, if multiple homes are enabled.
* `/delhome <name>` — Remove a saved home.
* `/homes` — View your saved homes.
* `/back` — Return to a previous location if supported. Do not assume this includes your death location.
* `/warps` and `/warp <name>` — List and visit public warp locations.
* `/rtp` — Request a random teleport where enabled.
* `/msg <player> <message>` — Send a private message.
* `/reply <message>` — Reply to your most recent private conversation.
* `/ignore <player>` — Hide messages from a player where supported.
* `/balance` — Check your balance in the current realm.
* `/cobbledollars pay <player> <amount>` — Send money if player payments are enabled. Double-check the recipient and amount.
* `/help`, `/rules`, and `/discord` — Look for command help, server rules, and the community link.

### Use teleport requests safely

Only accept requests from players you trust. Respect private bases and claims, and never use teleport commands to trap or harass someone.
