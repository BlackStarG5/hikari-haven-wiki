# 🌳 Safari

![Dedenne exploring a Safari habitat](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/f466de6249c5a9dc5906bcb2ef1c1db12644a2bb.png)

### A different kind of Pokémon hunt

The Safari is a dedicated dimension with **18 type-themed biomes**. Explore different habitats and make the most of your visit while searching for Pokémon.

### Before you head in

* **Bring Safari Balls:** Safari catches use these special balls.
* **Watch your time:** visits use a daily time allowance, with limits set by the server.
* **Be ready:** Pokémon can flee, so an encounter does not guarantee a catch.

Follow Hikari Haven’s current entrance instructions and check your supplies before beginning a visit.

### More places to discover

Depending on the enabled content, the adventure system also includes:

* **Hoopa portals:** temporary gateways to surprise destinations.
* **Sinnoh Underground:** excavation for spheres, fossils, and treasures, plus Pokémon hideaways.
* **Distortion World:** unusual exploration challenges, including parkour and mazes.
* **Pokémon nests:** wild eggs that use an Incubator.

### Plan your next trip

Check the available incense and perfume effects before using them; these can influence spawning. Locations, rewards, and access rules may vary with the server’s setup.

For deeper explanations of the underlying systems, visit the [official Safari wiki](https://cobblesafari.maxigregrze.fr/en).
