# 📜 Quests

### A little direction for your adventure

Quests turn activities such as catching, battling, training, and evolving Pokémon into goals you can follow. The quest system connects Cobblemon activities to **FTB Quests**.

### Following a quest

1. Open the **quest book or quest menu**. Check `Options → Controls` for its keybinding if you cannot find it.
2. Choose an available chapter and select a quest.
3. Read every task and prerequisite before starting. Some quests unlock only after earlier objectives are complete.
4. Carry out the listed activities and check your progress in the menu.
5. Claim any available rewards once the quest is complete.

### Possible objectives

* Catch specified Pokémon.
* Defeat or knock out wild Pokémon.
* Train Pokémon to a target level.
* Evolve a partner.
* Challenge supported NPC trainers.

### Read the quest details

Chapters, targets, and rewards are created for the server or modpack; the quest integration does not supply a complete adventure by itself. The quests visible in your menu are the ones currently available.

**Stuck?** check the exact task wording and any locked prerequisites first. If the objective still will not progress, share the quest name and a screenshot with staff.
