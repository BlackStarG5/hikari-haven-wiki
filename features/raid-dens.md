# ⚔️ Raid Dens

![A raid crystal marking a raid encounter](https://i.imgur.com/vkJYS5d.png)

### A challenge worth sharing

**Raid crystals** lead to encounters with powerful Pokémon in a separate raid dimension. Different tiers offer different challenges, so prepare your team before joining.

### Joining a raid

1. Find a raid crystal and review the encounter.
2. The first player to enter becomes the **host**.
3. Other players request to join; the host can accept or deny requests while the raid is active.
4. Choose your team carefully and coordinate with the other trainers.

### How the battle works

Players fight in separate, simultaneous battles, but the boss has **shared HP**. Your damage contributes to the group’s progress, so every participating trainer can help bring it down.

**Make your attempt count:** once your Pokémon have fainted, you cannot rejoin the fight. You can still receive rewards if the remaining players complete the raid.

### After a victory

A successful raid awards the raid Pokémon in your chosen ball and a **Raid Pouch** containing random loot. Available encounters, tiers, and additional rewards depend on the server’s setup.

**Trainer tip:** heal beforehand, consider the boss’s type matchups, and agree on a plan with your group before entering.
