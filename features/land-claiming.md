# 🏡 Land Claiming

### Make a place your own

Land claims reserve **chunks** of the world and apply protection settings to them. Use claims around your home, storage, and projects, then decide which friends should have access.

### Claiming land

1. Open the **Open Parties and Claims** menu using its keybinding. Find or change it in `Options → Controls`.
2. Use the claim interface to select available chunks within your allowance.
3. With the supported Xaero’s World Map integration, you can **right-click** a chunk or right-drag a selection to access claim actions.
4. Check the map boundaries and review your protection settings.

### Sharing with friends

Parties and access settings let you cooperate without opening everything to strangers. Read each permission before enabling it, especially interactions involving blocks, containers, and entities. Greyed-out settings are controlled by the server.

### Useful menu commands

Newer versions use `/oclaims` for claims, `/oparties` for parties, and `/opac` for player configuration. Use chat autocomplete to see the options available in the installed version.

**Remember:** claim limits and chunk loading depend on server settings. Claims do not replace server rules—respect other players’ builds, including unclaimed ones.
