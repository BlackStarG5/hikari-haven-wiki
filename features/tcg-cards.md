# 🃏 TCG Cards

![A Pokémon trading card displayed in Cobblemon Cards](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/9asBGJMf/images/66400790131c257af3e3d54820d33598b49ff7c7.gif)

### Your collection starts at the Hub

Visit **Hikari Haven’s Hub shop to buy card packs**. Open boosters to discover Pokémon cards, then build a collection around your favorite species, types, or generations.

### Collect and organize

* **Booster packs:** different packs focus on particular sets, generations, or types.
* **Card variants:** look out for different rarities and special artwork, including full-art shiny versions.
* **Binders:** keep cards together and browse your collection.
* **Card Cabinets:** store larger collections with search and sorting tools.

### More than a collection

Equipping a binder in the appropriate accessory slot can provide **passive bonuses**. Check the displayed effects and server settings before building around them.

A **Grading Station** grades cards from 1–10 and can affect their stat multipliers. A **Recycler** turns unwanted duplicates into **Cobblecard Dust**, giving spare pulls another purpose.

### Discover and display

The **Instant-Dex** system uses a Card Structure Disk and dust to scan wild Pokémon and compile cards. **Holo Projectors** let you display prized finds.

**Collector tip:** check your binder before recycling duplicates, and use the installed recipe viewer to find the equipment available in Hikari Haven’s version.
