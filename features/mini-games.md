# 🎮 Mini Games

![The arcade home menu; available games and costs can vary](https://i.imgur.com/Pr5zNxW.png)

### A little friendly competition

Arcade machines offer quick games with their own rules and rewards. Open a machine’s menu, choose an available game, and read its controls and entry cost before starting.

### What can you play?

* **Flappy Bird:** guide Butterfree through gaps.
* **Snake:** steer Onix toward berries without hitting walls or its own body.
* **Block Stacker:** time your placement to keep rows aligned.
* **Voltorb Flip:** use row and column clues to uncover multipliers while avoiding Voltorb.
* **Other supported games:** Roulette, Slot Machine, Derby, and Blackjack add more ways to play when enabled.

### Arcade Coins and your Coin Case

These games use **Arcade Coins**. A **Coin Case** stores a virtual balance and lets you deposit or withdraw physical coins. When you carry one, payouts go into the case first.

Check the reward shop to see what your coins can buy. Available games, costs, payouts, and shop rewards are set by the server.

### Play at your own pace

Start with a game you enjoy and learn its rules before spending more coins. Random outcomes never guarantee a particular reward. Arcade Coins are a separate system from CobbleDollars and the currencies used by other machines.
