# 📡 PokeNav

![PokeNav tracking helps point you toward a Pokémon in the world](https://i.imgur.com/y9iIKOx.png)

### Your exploration companion

**PokeNav** helps you understand local Pokémon spawns and search more deliberately. It is especially useful when you are exploring a new area or hunting a particular partner.

### Check the local possibilities

Use the spawn information to see which Pokémon **can appear at your current location**, their chances, and the conditions that influence them. Read those conditions before waiting in one spot.

**Important:** appearing in a spawn list does not mean a Pokémon is already nearby. It also does not guarantee that it will spawn during your visit.

### Find what is already there

* **Finder:** helps track spawned Pokémon using a directional 3D arrow.
* **PokéFinder:** provides a radar-style way to search.
* **FishingNav:** checks fishing-related spawn possibilities.

### Getting started

1. Look up the available navigation devices in your recipe viewer.
2. Read the device’s tooltip and open its interface.
3. Check `Options → Controls` for relevant keybindings if needed.
4. Use the information to choose where and when to explore.

Tracking helps you locate Pokémon; it does not summon them. Addon compatibility depends on whether those Pokémon use Cobblemon’s supported spawning system.
