# 💰 CobbleDollars

![Example merchant shop interface; stock and prices depend on the server](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/s7N7AsqL/images/aad21d9e40ec54c6f0e239bf838ed9862c858923.png)

### Your Pokémon adventure fund

**CobbleDollars** are an in-game currency used by merchants. Defeating wild Pokémon can award money, with the amount influenced by the Pokémon’s level and server settings.

### Buying from a merchant

1. **Right-click** a merchant to open its shop.
2. Browse categories or search for an item.
3. Choose the quantity and check the total before buying.
4. If the price is red, you do not have enough money for that purchase.

### Selling items

Use `Shift + right-click` on a merchant to open its bank interface. Merchants only buy items included in their configured trade list, so check what is accepted and the offered price before selling.

### Keep track of your balance

The money display helps you see what you can afford. Save up for something useful, and compare prices before spending your hard-earned balance.

**Realm reminder:** your items and Pokémon carry between Hikari Haven’s realms, but **money is separate between realms**.

**Different currencies:** do not assume CobbleDollars can replace arcade or gacha coins. Each activity may require its own currency; read the shop or machine’s instructions.
