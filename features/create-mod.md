# ⚙️ Create Mod

### Build something that moves

**Create** adds mechanical tools and animated contraptions for building, processing resources, and automating everyday jobs. Start with one useful machine, then grow your workshop as you learn.

### Learn by watching

1. Find a Create component in your inventory or the installed recipe viewer.
2. Look for its **Ponder** prompt. These in-game demonstrations show how supported components work and fit together.
3. Check the recipe viewer for the materials and processing steps you need.
4. Build a small test setup before connecting it to a larger project.

### Ideas for your first workshop

* A resource-processing corner beside your storage room.
* An automated production line for something you regularly use.
* A moving feature that gives your home, shop, or town some character.

### Exploring addons

Create addons can expand the available machines, materials, and building options. Browse the items and recipes in **Hikari Haven’s installed pack** to see what is actually available; recipes from a different pack may not match.

**Build thoughtfully:** keep your machines within your own space and follow server rules for automation. A compact, understandable workshop is easier to expand and troubleshoot.
