# 🧭 Expeditions

![Example Expedition Board showing missions and available Pokémon; server options may differ](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/f75656c718b16316357aa6b3fb94e347f2c5ffe6.png)

### Adventure beyond your active team

Expeditions let Pokémon from your **PC** take on timed missions while you continue your own journey. Your team’s strengths influence its chances and rewards.

### Send your first team

1. **Right-click an Expedition Board** and browse the available missions.
2. Select Pokémon from your PC. Review the mission hazards and success odds before dispatching them.
3. Wait for the expedition to finish, then return to the board to collect its rewards.

### Build a better expedition team

**Team Expedition Power (TEP)** measures your team’s suitability. Pokémon types, IVs, EVs, team size, and mission matchups all matter. A carefully chosen team can outperform one picked at random.

* **Outcomes:** great success, success, partial success, and failure affect what comes back.
* **Progression:** expedition rank and board tiers open further opportunities.
* **Fatigue:** give returning Pokémon time to recover and rotate teams.
* **Augments:** available upgrades can change speed, rewards, or mission odds.

### Useful commands

`/expedition check` — view active expeditions, remaining time, and odds.

`/expedition rewards` — check pending rewards; collect them at the board.

**Before dispatching:** read the displayed cost, duration, and reward information. These depend on the mission and server settings.
