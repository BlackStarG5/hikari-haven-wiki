# ⚔️ Battle Gimmicks

![A showcase of Pokémon transformations from Mega Showdown](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/SszvX85I/images/4844349ff28573c54f04e57ada01c554c064c886.png)

### Power up your battle plan

Hikari Haven features **Mega Evolution, Z-Moves, and Terastallization**. Each mechanic needs the right equipment and a compatible Pokémon, so prepare before challenging another trainer.

### The main gimmicks

* **Mega Evolution:** pair your trainer’s Key Stone accessory with the correct Mega Stone held by an eligible Pokémon. A species-specific stone matters—one stone does not work on every partner.
* **Z-Moves:** use a Z-Ring and a suitable Z-Crystal to unlock a special move for a compatible Pokémon and moveset.
* **Terastallization:** uses a Tera Orb and your Pokémon’s Tera Type to change how you approach type matchups.

### Getting ready

1. Read the transformation item’s tooltip and check which Pokémon it supports.
2. Equip trainer accessories in their appropriate slots and give your Pokémon its required held item.
3. Enter battle and look for the available gimmick control before choosing your move.

**Explore for upgrades:** Hikari Haven’s transformation items can be found through loot, including personal loot chests.

The mod also supports mechanics such as Dynamax, Gigantamax, and certain legendary transformations. Their availability depends on the server’s installed version and setup; follow event rules before using any gimmick competitively.
