# ✨ Pokeball Capsules

![Ball Capsule send-out effects in action](https://i.imgur.com/JAhsMox.gif)

### Make an entrance

**Ball Capsules** decorate the moment your Pokémon leaves its Poké Ball. Mix firework effects and stickers to create a look that suits your partner—bright, dramatic, or simply cute.

### Create your capsule

1. Obtain a **Ball Capsule** and the effects you want to use.
2. Combine the capsule with **Firework Stars**. Multiple stars let you build a more elaborate effect.
3. Add compatible **Stickers** for extra themed particles.
4. Use a **Capsule Station** to equip the finished capsule to your Pokémon.
5. Send your Pokémon out and enjoy its new introduction!

### Adding stickers

Sticker recipes use **Paper, Gunpowder, and an extra ingredient** that determines the effect. Type-themed stickers use type gems. Check your installed recipe viewer for the exact recipe and available choices.

You can combine stickers and firework stars in the same capsule, giving you room to experiment with different themes.

### Style tip

Try matching your effects to your Pokémon’s type, colors, or personality. Capsules change the visual send-out effect; they are a way to personalize your team rather than a battle upgrade.
