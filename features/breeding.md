# 🥚 Breeding

![An egg waiting to be collected from the pasture interface](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/ItmVb4zY/images/7f94c6013d99a1cc450be26899ee778fce5f0339.png)

### Start a Pokémon family

Breeding brings compatible Pokémon together in a **Pasture Block** to produce eggs. It is useful for collecting species and working toward the traits you want.

### Your first egg

1. Place compatible Pokémon in a pasture. Sharing a pasture alone does not guarantee compatibility.
2. Open the pasture interface and activate the **breeding button**.
3. Wait for an egg to appear in the nesting area at the bottom, then collect it.
4. Keep the egg in **your player inventory** while its hatching timer runs. Storing it elsewhere pauses that timer.

### Planning the next generation

* **Everstone:** helps pass down a parent’s nature.
* **Destiny Knot and Power items:** influence inherited IVs—the natural stats a Pokémon is born with.
* **Egg moves:** compatible moves can be inherited, including remembered moves outside the active moveset.
* **Other traits:** abilities, Poké Balls, and forms can also follow inheritance rules.

**Good to know:** compatible Pokémon belonging to different players can breed together. Agree with friends beforehand about who collects the eggs! Breeding times and special chances depend on server settings.
